## Reusable records and isolation

Each indexed compressed shard contains up to 16 whole BO3 records. Three separate components store (1) full ordered legal callback observations/events and responses, including forced selections; (2) aligned hidden hand/draw/prize counts plus physical hand serials at up to 24 hash-selected genuine decisions/game; and (3) policy/adapter/SDK/deck/schema provenance, chooser, actual starters, outcomes and timing. Raw legal inputs contain no diagnostic targets. All library card IDs define the query universe, independently of the actual hidden hand. Target joins verify native pre-action state keys, public counts and physical known-card bounds.

New collection uses the same frozen actors. The observer runs after collection, and its corrected Metang/Dwebble/Crispin/Drakloak/provenance semantics are reused unchanged. All old fact/reset/deduplication checks remain. The exact marginal-distribution extension is checked against the frozen A presence/mean/variance implementation at every labelled training/validation point. A material correctness error stops collection, preserving the failed ID and all in-flight attempts. No public count is overwritten to fit truth.

Locked-test integrity checks only verify schema, alignment, legal count bounds, frozen policy and schedule identity. The model dataset loader rejects locked-test inputs and targets, and preprocessing rejects that partition. Test targets are sealed after collection. No test model scores, outcome exploration or model selection are authorized. A valid raw dataset does not imply that model fitting or validation has finished.

Original full dataset complete: **False**. Committed records/eligible-subset integrity verified: **True**. Test committed: **592/592**; test model scores: **0**. Whole-BO3 grouping is preserved for every view and game.

The raw archive supports future legal-history encoders and alternative marginal count targets. Its sampled target points do not provide labels at arbitrary unrecorded decisions, and the records do not establish counterfactual simulator support. Derived marginal predictions are not automatically joint hidden-world samples.
